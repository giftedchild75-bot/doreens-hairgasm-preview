Choosing your first unit

Lace, length, texture, colour and care, in plain words. Still unsure after reading? We'll help you choose on WhatsApp.

Lace & closures

The lace is the part that sits at your parting or hairline, so it decides how you can wear your hair. The numbers are its size in inches: width, then depth.

2x6 closure
A strip of lace 2 inches wide and 6 inches deep down the centre. Made for a sharp, natural middle part — the look on our bone straight units.
5x5 closure
A 5-by-5-inch square of lace at the crown. Room to part in the middle or slightly to either side.
13x4 frontal
Lace from ear to ear, 4 inches back from the hairline. Lets you part anywhere along the front and style the edges.
13x6 frontal
The same ear-to-ear width, 6 inches deep — more space for deep side parts and pulled-back styles.

Rule of thumb: a closure is the simplest to wear; a frontal gives the most styling freedom.

Length

Lengths are measured in inches, straight, from the top of the unit to the ends. Where they fall on you depends on your height and how the unit is cut.

10"–12"
Bob territory — around the jaw to the chin.
14"–16"
Around the collarbone to the shoulders.
18"–22"
Past the shoulders to mid-back.
24" and longer
Mid-back and beyond — the dramatic end of the range.

Curls and waves sit shorter than the same length worn straight, because the pattern takes up length.

Texture & fringe

Bone straight is sleek and flat from root to tip. Bouncy styles have soft, loose movement. Pixie curls are tight, defined curls with plenty of volume.

A fringe (bangs) frames the face and hides the hairline, so there's no lace to blend at the front — a good choice if you're new to wigs. Where a style comes with or without a fringe, you pick on the product page.

Colour

Every colour you can select on a product page is in our price list. Some pieces come in more colours than we list for checkout — the description says which, and you can order those on WhatsApp.

A dark root means the hair is darker at the parting and lighter through the lengths, which softens the line between the lace and your scalp.

Screens show colour differently. If the exact shade matters, ask us for a photo or video of the unit in daylight before you order.

Care

  • Detangle gently with a wide-tooth comb, starting at the ends and working up.
  • Wash only when it needs it, with a gentle shampoo and conditioner. Let it dry on a wig stand, not in a heap.
  • For curls, refresh with water and a leave-in conditioner instead of brushing them dry.
  • Store your unit on a stand or in its box, away from direct sun.
  • Ask us before using heat. The safe temperature depends on the unit, and we'll tell you for your piece.
