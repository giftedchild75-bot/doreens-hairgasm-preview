Before You Order

Common Questions

Is this human hair?

We confirm hair type piece by piece. Message us on WhatsApp with the item you're eyeing and we'll tell you its hair type and density before you pay.

Which length should I choose?

Lengths are measured straight, in inches. As a guide, 10" is a bob, 14" sits near the shoulders and 18"+ falls down the back. Curls sit shorter. Length guide

Can I get a colour that isn't listed?

Some pieces come in more colours than we list for checkout — the product description names them. Message us on WhatsApp to order one of those.

How do I order and pay?

Message us on WhatsApp with the piece you like and we'll confirm availability, delivery and payment details with you directly.

Do you deliver?

Yes — we ship nationwide within Nigeria and internationally. Delivery options and fees for your address are shown at checkout, or message us for a quote.

Can I customize the color, length, or style?

Yes — that's exactly what a Custom Install is for. Send a reference photo or describe the vibe, and we'll style it to match.

How long will my unit last?

It depends on the unit and how it's cared for. Our care guide covers the basics, and we'll tell you what to expect from the specific piece you're considering.
