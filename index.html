Before You Order

Common Questions

Is this human hair?

Every piece has full specs available — message us on WhatsApp with the item you're eyeing and we'll confirm hair type, length, and density before you order.

How much does it cost?

Every wig shows its price on the product page. Custom installs and styling services are quoted on WhatsApp, because they depend on what you'd like done.

How do I order and pay?

Message us on WhatsApp with the piece you like and we'll confirm availability, delivery and payment details with you directly.

Do you deliver?

Yes — we ship nationwide within Nigeria and internationally. Delivery options and fees for your address are shown at checkout, or message us for a quote.

Can I customize the color, length, or style?

Yes — that's exactly what a Custom Install is for. Send a reference photo or describe the vibe, and we'll style it to match.

How long will my install last?

It depends on the unit and how it's cared for. Message us about the specific piece you're considering and we'll walk you through maintenance and what to expect.
